This showcased an extension of some Wi-Fi frames for dual-WAN STAs such that the STA could send cell telemetry to the access point/gateway, allowing the Wi-Fi network to make more intelligent client steering decisions. This directly lead to a Wi-Fi Alliance adoption/spec change of the Cellular Data Connectivity field found in the MBO Information Element in IEEE802.11 Association, Reassociation, and Probe Requests to move from a binary yes/no to a full octect representing a spectrum of possible cell connectivity metrics. tl;dr: prototype demo lead to a spec change for every Wi-Fi powered device globally.
